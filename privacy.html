I only collect data that you voluntarily provide to me. If you choose to sign up for the newsletter, the only information collected is:

3. Your Data Rights & Control

You maintain full control over your information. At any point, you have the right to:

If you have any questions or would like to request changes to your data records, please reach out directly via email at: contact@studiotroman.com.
